PROBLEM STATEMENT
  • Lack of Transparency: Donors have no visibility into how or where their funds are utilized.
  • Uneven Distribution: Manual aid allocation results in over-funding some areas while ignoring critical needs elsewhere.
  • Verification Delays: Identifying fake vs. genuine requests manually is slow and prone to human error.
2
OBJECTIVES
  • Automate the verification of relief requests using AI to detect spam and assess credibility.
  • Implement a mathematical Relief Priority Index (RPI) to rank requests objectively.
  • Develop a Smart Allocation Engine to mathematically divide donations.
3
PROPOSED SOLUTION

ReliefAid bridges Donors directly with verified Focal Persons.

  • AI Verification: Field requests are processed by Google Gemini AI which scores credibility and flags inconsistencies.
  • Smart Distribution: A custom RPI algorithm ranks verified requests. A greedy algorithm automatically assigns incoming funds to the most critical needs.

9
NOVELTY & CONTRIBUTION

The major innovation is the Relief Priority Index (RPI) algorithm.

Instead of blind flat-rate distribution, it computes a dynamic score (0-100) based on AI credibility, funding gaps, affected families, and urgency, ensuring completely data-driven aid distribution.
